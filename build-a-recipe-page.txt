<h1>Fudgy Chocolate Chip Brownies</h1>
<p>The perfect fudgy chocolate chip brownies full of guilt and chocolate.</p>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
  <li>milk</li>
  <li>eggs</li>
  <li>flour</li>
  <li>cocoa powder</li>
  <li>vanillla</li>
 <li> salt</li>
 <li> oil</li>
 <li> dark chocolate chips </li>
</ul>
<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li> preheat oven to 325 F </li>
<li> mix ingredients into a bowl </li>
<li> pour into 8x8 pan </li>
<li> cook for 40-48 minutes until toothpick in center comes out clear </li>
<li> allow to cool completely </li>
  </ol>
